# Cycode

> Cycode is a complete Application Security Posture Management (ASPM) and software supply chain security platform providing visibility, security, and integrity across the entire SDLC. Its Risk Intelligence Graph (RIG) correlates SAST, SCA, secrets, IaC, and container findings. Cycode ships a REST API, an official CLI, and an official MCP server for AI-assisted scanning.
